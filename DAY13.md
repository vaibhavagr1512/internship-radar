# Day 13 — Deadline filters

The tracker adds All deadlines, Due today, Next 7 days (including today), Past deadline, and No recorded deadline.

Today is the device's local calendar date. The next-seven-day window includes today through today plus six calendar days, avoiding daylight-saving hour arithmetic. Past deadline excludes today. Unknown deadlines are included only by All deadlines and No recorded deadline. All statuses may be filtered; Past deadline describes the listing's deadline, not a claim that an already-applied application is late.

The date updates every minute and on window focus. Deadline filters combine with role/company search and application status. Reset application view restores all statuses, all deadlines, blank search and newest-updated sorting; the chosen page size remains.

Deadlines are date-only information; confirm actual closing time and eligibility on the official listing. Demo dates are illustrative. See DAY15.md for installation and checks.
